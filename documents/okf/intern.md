# Intern Policy

General Intern Policy and Guidelines
ACCESS LEVEL: PUBLIC / INTERN TIER 
TIER 1 - INTERN
Welcome to NovaTech Innovations. This handbook serves as the foundational guide for all interns joining our
organization across various technical, product, and functional departments. As an intern, you play a vital role in
supporting our daily team objectives while acquiring hands-on industry experience in a dynamic workplace.
1. Intern Onboarding
Your onboarding process officially begins on your first day with a company-wide orientation session conducted by
Human Resources. During this session, you will receive your corporate IT credentials, workstation setup instructions,
and initial security provisioning for our internal developer portal and learning systems.
Following HR orientation, you will meet with your assigned buddy and direct supervisor. They will walk you through the
team workspace, introduce key stakeholders, and outline your preliminary deliverables for the first two weeks. All
onboarding checklists must be completed and submitted in the Intern Portal within your first five business days.
2. Intern Responsibilities
Interns are expected to actively participate in team activities, demonstrate continuous learning, ask questions when
blocked, and deliver quality contributions under mentor supervision. Core responsibilities include:
Assisting software engineers and product teams with daily operational tasks, codebase maintenance, and
documentation.
Writing modular, well-tested code or preparing draft technical reports based on given specifications.
Attending daily stand-up meetings, weekly team synchronization meetings, and monthly intern cohort check-ins.
Adhering strictly to version control conventions, code formatting standards, and internal security protocols.
Logging completed tasks, time allocations, and daily progress notes in the project management board.
3. Leave Policy
Interns are eligible for a basic leave allowance designed to accommodate essential personal needs, academic
commitments, and medical issues. During a standard six-month internship term, interns accrue 1 day of paid personal
leave per month worked, totaling up to 6 days for the full term.
To request personal or medical leave, interns must adhere to the following protocol:
Submit an official leave request through the HR Portal at least 48 hours in advance for planned personal absences.
For unexpected illness, notify your direct supervisor and HR via email before 9:00 AM on the day of absence.
Unused leave does not carry over beyond the duration of your internship agreement and cannot be converted into
cash payout.
Note on Overlapping Policy: Leave allowances for interns are set at 1 day/month (max 6 days). Additional PTO
entitlements apply only to regular employees, managers, and executive staff.

5. Learning and Training
Learning and professional development are central to our internship program. Interns receive access to NovaTech’s
internal e-learning catalog, which includes curated video tracks for software engineering practices, cloud
fundamentals, product design, data analytics, and project management.
Interns are expected to dedicate approximately 4 hours per week toward assigned self-paced learning modules. Upon
completing basic tracks, interns can request access to specialized vendor certification paths after discussion with
their assigned mentor.
6. Mentorship
Every intern is paired with a dedicated mentor who is a mid-level or senior professional within the same department.
Your mentor serves as your primary guide for professional development, technical guidance, code reviews, and career
advice.
Mentors conduct weekly 30-minute one-on-one sessions to review progress, provide constructive feedback on
deliverables, address technical blockers, and help navigate organizational dynamics. Interns are encouraged to come
prepared to these sessions with questions and discussion topics.
7. Attendance & Hours
Consistent attendance and punctuality are essential for a successful internship experience. Core business hours for
all employees and interns are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a standard one-hour lunch break.
If you anticipate being late or absent due to an emergency, notify your manager and mentor as early as possible.
Repeated unexcused tardiness or absence may lead to a formal review and potential termination of the internship
agreement.
8. Internship Evaluation
Intern progress is assessed at two key intervals during the program: a mid-term review (at week 12) and a final
comprehensive evaluation (at week 24). Evaluations are conducted by your direct supervisor with qualitative input
from your assigned mentor.
The evaluation criteria focus on five core pillars:
Technical competency, problem-solving ability, and work quality.
Initiative, self-motivation, and adaptability to feedback.
Collaboration, written and verbal communication, and team integration.
Timeliness and dependability in completing assigned project milestones.
High-performing interns who receive exemplary ratings on their final evaluation may be considered for full-time junior
positions or extended internship terms upon completion of their academic studies.
9. Project Guidelines
